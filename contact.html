FAQ

Before we start.

Clear expectations make better projects.

How much does a website cost?

Website projects start at $300 CAD. Final pricing depends on scope, content, functionality and integrations.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes, where appropriate. Self-editing can be planned for selected content such as services, images, testimonials and contact information.

Can I preview the website before it goes live?

Yes. You can review a private preview before the final domain is connected.

What are Event & QR Solutions?

They can include event pages, registration, RSVP, QR check-in, digital tickets, access cards and feedback flows.

Do you only work with Winnipeg businesses?

No. I am based in Winnipeg and can work remotely with businesses across Canada.
